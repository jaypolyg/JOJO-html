Johnny Joestar: The Spin's Chosen

Johnny Joestar is the protagonist of the seventh part of JoJo's Bizarre Adventure. He starts his journey as a tragic figure, marked by a crippling injury and a deep sense of guilt. Despite his initial cynicism and selfishness, Johnny's relation with Gyro Zeppeli leads him to transform into a a true hero,seen by the evolution of his Stand Tusk,named after the band.

-Significance

As the protagonist of part 7 the story follows arround his quest to defeat the personal demons that plauge him after his dibilitating accident that left him paralysed.As part 7 is the first part of the original timeline it falls to Johnny to recreate the JoJo matle of heroism that was passed on through generations in the previous parts.
His most defining actions are defeating his part's main antagonist Funny Valentine aswell as acting as the story's thematic heart with his relation with his father .

Gyro Zeppeli:The smile of God

Gyro is the secondary protagonist of part 7.He is a master of the Spin and an executioner hailing from the Kingdom of Naples. He joins the Steel Ball Run race to win amnesty for a child he is assigned to execute.

-Significance

Gyro's significance to the story comes from his relationship with Johnny as he starts from his mentor in the art of the Spin and eventually becomes his close friend. Just like Johnny, Gyro has a personal journey of equal significance as his personal motivation and backstory slowly unravel throughout the part
His most defining action is mentoring Johnny in the spin and helping him kill Funny Valentine with the Golden Spin.

Funny Valentine: He who holds the Napking

Valentine is the main antagoinst of Part 7.He is a cunning and ruthless politician who seeks to obtain the "Saint's Corpse," a powerful artifact that grants immense power. His ultimate goal is to reshape the world to his vision.He posses the stand D4C,named after the AC/DC song "Dirty Deeds Done Dirt Cheap" aswell as the power "Love Train",named after the song from the O'Jays.

-Significance

Valentine plays a significant role throughout the part both as an antagoinstic force and as well as a philosophical, as Johnny's journey and his are intertwined leading to their philosophical conflict as different worldviews and opinions of both humanity and the relations between sons and fathers collide.
His most defining action is killing Gyro Zeppeli aswell as acting as a great philosophical contrast to the protagonists and acting like a peek to the politcal world of JoJo's.

Josuke Higashikata: The sailor adrift

Josuke is the protagonist of Part 8 and shares the same name with the protagonist of Part 4.He is the stand user of "Soft and Wett",named after the Prince song. As an amneseac he embarks on a quest with the help of Yasuho,his love interest, to discover his past

-Significance

As a protagonist the story follows Josuke and his adventure in disovering his past, as the fusion two different men Kira Yosikage and Josefumi Kujo.His story is intertwined with the Locacaca fruit catalyst to his transformation.
His most defining action is stopping the Locacaca trade by being integral in the death of all the members of the Locacaca Organisation.

Toru/Satoru Akefu: Calamity Incarnate

Toru is the hidden leader of the Locacaca Organisation and acts as the main antagonist of part 8.He is the stand user of "Wonder of U",named after the Elvis song"Wonder of You" a stand that influences the force of Calamity.

-Significance

He is theRock Human mastermind behind the Locacaca Organization under the guise of his Stand, which he disguises as Satoru Akefu, the director of TG University Hospital. Toru himself masquerades as an ordinary part-time medical student at the hospital. Toru's main motive is to develop a revolutionary medicine through the New Locacaca with the goal of restructuring the balance of power in society.
His most defining action is acting as threatening presence aswell as a great mystery at the second half of part 8.
