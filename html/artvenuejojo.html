The Louvre Invites The Comics

Here’s a cleaned-up version of your text with typos corrected: During this event, a series of comic books and manga were showcased in the Louvre. This event was organized by Fabrice Douar and opened twice: once from January 22 to April 13, 2009, and again from January 28 to February 28, 2010, at the Louvre Museum. Hirohiko Araki JoJo Exhibition: Ripples of Adventure

This event was held as a celebratory special exhibition in honor of JoJo's Bizarre Adventure's 30th anniversary. It was originally held from August 24 to October 1, 2018, at the National Art Center in Tokyo, Japan. The exhibition was held three additional times: from November 24 to January 14, 2019, in Osaka; from January 25 to March 29, 2019, in Nagasaki; and finally from April 30 to May 28, 2022. As a celebratory event, this exhibition featured new artwork for some of the oldest characters in the series, rendered in Araki's updated and refined art style. It also showcased many of Araki's iconic characters in different fashionable attires with striking coloring. During the first press conference for this event, the animated adaptation of Part 5: Vento D'Auro was also revealed
