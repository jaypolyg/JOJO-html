Giorno Giovana: The Golden Son

Giorno is the main protagonist of JoJo's Part 5:Vento D'Auro.He is a son of Dio that has chosen to involve himself with the world of the Italian mafia and the group Passione in order to stop the circulation of drugs to underage children and teenagers. He is the stand user of "Golden Experience",named after the Prince song"Gold",a stand that can give and manipulate life.

-Significance

Giorno is a person that embodies many qualities of both Jonathan and Dio.Giorno's main characteristic is his unrivaled resolve throught the entirety of the Part as he ruthlessly seeks to become the leader of Passione as he constantly dissregards his own health in order to obtain victory.It is also shown that he is incretibly vindictive against those who would harm the innocent,making sure they die in horrible and gruesome ways.He is exceptionally observative of the people arround him and treats people with the respect their beliefs merrit.Being saved as a child from his abussive parents by a gangster whom he saved Giorno learns to trust people from him.Eventually Giorno obtains the Power of the requiem Arrow that evolves his stand into "Golden Experience Requiem" a symbolic stand that is implied to repressent the whims of fate and punishes Diavolo for evading his fated demise with an endless death loop.Giorno then becomes both the guardian of the Requiem Arrow aswell as the leader of Passione.His fate after the universal reset is left ambiguous.
His most defining action is defeating Diavolo,the primary antagoinst of Part 5 and stopping the underage drug trade in Italy.

Diavolo/Doppio Vinegr: Satan and Lucifer

Diavolo is the main antagonist of JoJo's Part 5:Vento D'Auro.He is the child of a miracle birth as his mother was imprissioned for two years in an all woman's prision and became the leader of the italian mafia gang Passione after becoming the stand user of "King Crimson"and "Epitaph",named after the rock band King Crimson and their song Epitaph,a stand that changes fate as it erases time.He is an extremely twisted and evil person,as even a youth he bound and burried his mother under his room and when discovered he burned the entire village where he lived to conseal his tracks.

-Significance

Driven to obtain the Requiem Arrow Diavolo will go to any and all extremes in order to obtain it.Characterised by his obsession with erasing his existance from history and keeping his identity hidden,he considers the past to be his greatest enemy that he must best.As such he believes results are the only things that trully matter in the world making him and his stand King Crimson's power thematic parallels as it erasses the immideate future so that only the fate that benefits him remains.He is an extremely egotistical and pridefull man who considers even having a daughter a weakness he must destroy.Despite that it seems that he is able of expressing affection as he habitually calls his alter ego Doppio as "My cute Doppio" and urges him to safety away from Nero,aswell as harboring some form of love to his past lover since he never sent anyone to assassinate her and seem to have fealings for her,atleast for the time they spent together.Diavolo eventually finds his end to the hands of Giorno as he is pummeled to death by Golden Experience and is forced to exprerience an infinite death loop as punishment for his crimes.
His most defining action is being the catalyst of the events of the part as Giorno's crusade against him happend due to the callousness with which he treated human lifes.

Bruno Bucciarati: Dead Man Walking

Bucciarati is the secondary protagonist of JoJo's Part 5:Vento D'Auro.He is a high ranking mafioso who meets Giorno Giovana and shares the same goal of uprooting the corruption Diavolo has brought into Passione.He is the stand user of "Sticky Fingers",named after the album with the same name from band Metallica, a stand that allows the user to put stickers into anything.Suffering due to the drug trade that took his father's life after a messy divorse at one point he had to kill a mobster who would kill his father.

-Significance

As Bucciarati is the secondary protagonist he is present all throughout the part and is a constant combatant against enemy stand users. Despite the conditions that forced him into a life of crime Bucciarati is a righteous and kind man that seems to deeply care for the good of the common people.During fights Bucciarati acts with great caution and is quick to sacrifice himself in order to win.He appears to value deeply familly as a construct and is clearly disgusted with Diavolo for even thinking about harming his daughter let alone being the one to try and kill her calling him the definition of nausiating evil.Despite dying to protect Trish from Diavolo he clings to life as a spirit thanks to the help of Golden Experience he still does all that he can to achieve his goal and eventually passes away at the final battle ascending into heaven leaving Giorno with a bittersweet goodbye
His most defining action is being the leader of the main group aswell as acting as Diavolo's thematic foil as he is a man who cherises the connections he has with others and believes family to be of great importance.

Risotto Nero: Leader of La Squadra

Risotto is the secondary main antagonist of JoJo's Part 5:Vento D'Auro.He is a high ranking mobster in the group Passione who turns traitor to the organisation with his team "La Squadra Esecuzioni" and seeks to uncover Diavolo's identity.He is the stand user of "Metallica",named after the band Metallica, a stand that allows the user to manipulate metal through magnetism.

-Significance

After becoming a mafioso at the age of 18 he and his team eventually became dissatisfied in their role inside Passione as they were largly distrusted by Diavolo and never were allowed territorial claims.Due to this feeling two members of his team Sorbet and Gelato sought out to find who Diavolo was.This lead to the team discovering their remains as one died from asphexiation and the other was chopped into pieces and preserved in formalin.After this event they decided to use Trish as leverage in order to take revenge against Diavolo.The squad member of the team act as the minor antagonists through the first and second thirds of part 5. Risotto is a stoic and driven character that will go to any lenghts to find out about Diavolo and have his revenge.He eventually faces off against Doppio who he eventually forces into transforming into Diavolo in order to survive the fight.Despite his little time in the spotlight he is a character of great importance to the events of Part 5 as his death acts as a turning point in the story
His most defining action is being the leader of La Squadra Esecuzioni who serve as secondary antagonists through the first half of part 5.
