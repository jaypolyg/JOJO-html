Jotaro Kujo: The strongest man

Jotaro Kujo is a recurring character as the main protagonist of Part 3:Stardust Crusaders aswell as a primary character in Part 4:Diamon is Unbreakable and Part 6:Stone Ocean. He is the Stand user of "Star Platinum",named after the tarrot card "The Star", a powerfull close combat stand with the ability to stop time up to 5 seconds. Despite his introduction as a rough delinquent and his introverted exterior he is a kind man who will go to any lenghts to protect his familly.He is inspired by Clint Eastwood and acts like him.

-Significance

Jotaro is one of the most significant characters of JoJo as he is both the protagonist of a Part and the one to kill the Joestar bloodline's greatest foe,Dio but he is also shown to be a great leader and mentor in the later Parts Despite his strained relationship with his daughter he deeply cares for her to the point were he choses to save her at the finalle of part 6 instead of attacking Pucci at the Maid in Heaven arc,leading to his death and the reset at the universe.He is again reincarnated in the new timeline,having a better relationship with his daughter as she seems exited to see him with her boyfriend.
His most defining action is killing Dio and settling the blood debt of the Joestar Bloodline.

Jean Pierre Polnareff: The french Clown

Polnareff is a reccuring character in JoJo's Part3:Stardust Crusaders as a primary character and Part 5:Vento D'Auro as a secondary ally.He is the stand user of "Silver Chariot" ,named after the Tarrot Card "The Chariot", a close ranged stand that specialises in tremendous speed. Driven by revenge,he partners with the Crusaders in order to kill Dio aswell as the man who killed his sister.

-Significance

Initially introduced as a foe that was under Dio's mind controll Polnareff is an invaluable ally to the Joestars in their quest to kill Dio and is one of the three Crusaders to survive the adventure. He also later appears in Part 5 as a secondary ally to Giorno who reveals the identity of the antagonist Diavolo to him aswell as setting the stage for the final confrontation with him due to his evolved stand silver chariot requiem.
His most defining action is killing Vanilla Ice, Dio's second in command as well as helping Jotaro in the fight against Dio .

Enya the Hag

Enya is a primary antagoinst in Part 3:Stardust Crusaders. She also is a stand user of "Justice" ,named after the same tarrot card, that creates illusions aswell as controll people through their injuries.Despite trying to play to her old and frail appearance she is killed when the Crusaders encounter her by Dio's flesh bud.

-Significance

She has a great impact in the overall story as the one who provided Dio with the stand arrow, as well as the reason for the increase in the stand user popullation as she is the one who distributed the varrious stand arrows throughout the world.As such she is directly responsible for the Morioh Stand users that appear throught Part 4 aswell as the one who provided Passione with the stand arrow aswell as directly awakening Diavolo's stand "King Crimson"
Her most defining action is the spread of the Stand Arrow and the increase of the stand popullation .

Josuke Higashikata: The heart of Diamonds

Josuke is the main protagonist of JoJo's Part 4:Diamond is Unbreakable. He is the illegitimate son of Joseph Joestar and a resident of Morioh. He is the stand user of "Crazy Diamond", named after the Pink Floyd song "Shine On You Crazy Diamond", with the power to heal almost anything. His iconic appearance and posing is inspired by Prince during his New Power Generation era.Despite being seen as a delinquent due to his fashion sence aswell as his attitude, Josuke is a deeply kind person seen by his stand's primary power

-Significance

As the protagonist the story of Part 4 follows him and his whacky adventures inside the small but crazy town of Morioh where he befriends and defeats various stand users.Eventually during his adventures in Morioh he and his friends pursue a local serial killer and eventually defeat him bringing peace to the town
His most defining action is the actions he took throughout the part to defeat Kira Yoshikage, the main antagoinst.

Kira Yoshikage: The Unseen Explosive Evil

Kira is the primary antagoinst of Part 4:Diamond is Unbreakable.He is a serial killer,with a fixation on hands, who existed in Morioh for many years undisturbed in his life.Kira is the stand user of "Killer Queen" that also posses two different named powers called "Sheer Heart Attack" and "Bites the Dust",named after the same songs of the band Queen, that specialises in explosions.He exhibits many egotistical,psycopathic and obsessive-compulsive tendancies and great murderous compulsions.Appart from his murders to satisfy his extreme hand fetish,Kira is extremely violent and sadistic against any who whould threaten his quiet lifestyle and attract attention to him.His appearance is inspired by David Bowie.

-Significance

Eventually as the ghost of his first victim Reimy Sugimoto informs Josuke and his friends about his existance his life is filled with turmoil up to the point where with the help with another stand user takes over another man's life to use as cover.Despite all his efforts he is eventually found by his new son Hayato Kawajiri as a serial killer impersonating his father leading to his demise.After his death Kira is dragged into the underworld by the suppernatural power of the ghost alley where he lives a new life as a ghost. Post-mortem in the Deadman's Question Kira no longer has his impulsive tendancies however he retains his desire for a peacefull existance.
His most defining action is taking over the entire Plot through his ability "Bites the Dust" and changing the genre of the series to a crime thriller while he is centered in the story.

Hirose Koichi: The Hero's Jurney

Koichi is a secondary protagonist in Part 4:Diamond is Ubreakable and a secondary character in Part 5:Vento D'Auro.He is the stand user of "Echoes",named after the Pink Floyd song "Echoes",that manipulates sound.He also acts as the gateway to the begining of the story as he is the narrator of part 4.

-Significance

Koichi starts off as a meek but good intending youth, however due to his involvment in the stand world he grows as a person as he becomes courageous and strong willed. This can be seen both litterally,as his stand Echoes transforms to match his resolve, and metaphorically as he becomes more assertive and develops a functional relationship despite it's rocky beginings.Koichi serves as the fourth part's secondary protagonist,as he undergoes the typical Hero's Jurney throughout the part.Starting from an outsider to the stand world he experiences the steps of the trope throughout the story. At first he is terrified of the stand world and his willpower is so weak that his stand is simply an egg,revealing his potentional.Throught his journey he experiences the typical "Death & Rebirth" that is associated witht the trope three times as he evolves in the face of opposition and changes his mentality.Koichi also makes a brief appearance in Part 5, where he acts as the reader's/viewer's first look into the protagonist of the Part,Giorno.
His most defining actions are his narrative arc aswell as the actions he takes against Kira Yoshikage.

Rohan Kishibe: The Author's Favorite Child

Rohan is a primary character in Part 4:Diamond is Ubreakable and the protagonist of his own spinoff series "Thus Spoke Kishibe Rohan" aswell as a secondary character in Part 9:JoJoLands.He is a successfull manga artist and the stand user of "Heaven's Door",named after the Guns and Roses song "Knockin'On Heaven's Door", a stand that makes people into books one can write into.

-Significance

Rohan is introduced as an antagonist that pursues inspiration for his manga in the most outlandish of ways,he quickly grows into an ally due to his fondness of Koichi and his personality.He is an artist through and through,uninterested in money and fame,he pursues the continuation of his series through the most genuine and burning passion,seeking new inspiration no matter the cost.As a person despite his arrogant and eccentrick demeanor he is a good person and by the time of his spinoff series has matured enough to know that despite that being better than others in his craft is easy,surpassing himself is far a greater task.Rohan makes an appearance in Part 9 where he travels in Hawai for vacation and inspiration but ends up investigating the mysterious Lava Rock and it's ability to attract objects of vallue and has an eventfull encounter with the main cast of the part.
His most defining action is being so popullar that he got his own spinoff series and even appears in the new timeline completly unchanged.

Jolyne Kujo: The Stone cast Ashore

Jolyne is the main protagonist of JoJo's Part 6:Stone Ocean.She is the daughter of Jotaro Kujo.She is the user of the stand "Stone Free",named after the song "Stone Free" from Jimi Henderix, a stand that allows the user to unravel themselves into thread.

-Significance

As the protagonist of Part 6 the story follows her in her quest to escape prision.She is framed for DUI and is sent to a maximum sequrity prision.She is confrontational and driven,vowing to make her ex boyfriend pay for lying in court.After being thrown into the stand world and confronting many life and death situations she develops an incretible ammount of resolve, as she chaces Enrico Pucci and vows to save her father.She is incretably vindictive yet deeply righteous as she inspires those arround her while stoichaly facing any opposition.She even resolves herself to die for the sake of a child,Emporio, that ends up being the reason for the ultimate demise of Enrico Pucci.She is later ressurected in the new timeline as Erine and is the girlfriend of Anasui,now called Anakiss, and seems to have a far better relationship with her father in the new timeline.
Her most defining action is saving Emporio's life who ends up killing Enrico Pucci reseting the timeline once more to it's intended course as a new univers.

Enrico Pucci:The world's Gravity

Pucci is the main antagonist of JoJo's Part 6:Stone Ocean.He is the separated at birth brother of Weather Report,another main character, and works as prission priest.He is the stand user of three different stands: "Whitesnake" named after the rock band with the same name,"C-MOON" named after the song C Moon by the Wings and "Made in Heaven",named after the Queen song with the same name.

-Significance

As a loyal friend of Dio,Pucci plots and fulfills their long desired plan to achieve Heaven.Armed with a steady resolve as well as great cunning.He stopped at nothing to achieve his plan and was ruthless all throughout considering any and all sacrifices to his plan as insignificant towards the greater good.As a man while inherently good hearted his desired to do good was twisted by Dio as their friendship flourished and he became resolved to see the plan of Heaven through.Eventually achieving his goal and killing nearly all of his enemies and reseting the main JoJo's timeline he is eventually killed by Emporio using his brother's stand weather report leading to another universal reset.
His most defining action is reseting the timeline and giving birth to the last trilogy of parts.
